# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 2026

At **Autopost**, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and handle your information when you use our dashboard.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

#### a) Authentication & Account Data
When you connect your social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube) via OAuth:
- We receive public profile information (such as your username/handle) and authentication tokens provided by the respective platforms.
- **We never store or request your social media passwords.**

#### b) Media & Upload Data
Files (images, videos) dropped into the dashboard are processed solely to fulfill your request to post them to your selected platforms. 

#### c) Technical & Usage Data
We may automatically log basic browser information (e.g., user agent, IP address) necessary to run and secure the web application interface.

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use your data exclusively to:
- Authenticate your session and display your connected account status.
- Upload and publish media content to your selected platforms on your behalf.
- Ensure the technical stability and security of the Autopost service.

### 3. Data Sharing & Third Parties
We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Your data is only shared with third-party APIs (TikTok Inc., Meta Platforms Inc., Google LLC / YouTube) as required to perform the auto-posting actions you trigger.

### 4. Data Storage & Retention
Authentication tokens are stored securely and are only used for active sessions. You can revoke Autopost's access at any time through the dashboard or directly in your third-party account settings (e.g., Google Security Settings, TikTok App Permissions).

### 5. Your Rights
Under applicable data protection laws (such as GDPR), you have the right to:
- Access the personal data held about you.
- Request the deletion or correction of your data.
- Disconnect your social media accounts at any time.

### 6. Contact & Support
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please open an issue in this GitHub repository or contact the project maintainer.
